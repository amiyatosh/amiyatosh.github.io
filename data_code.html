Data & Code

Datasets and replication packages from my published work. If you use any of these, please cite the paper the material comes from.

Replication package: United They Fall

Gandhi, P., and A. Purnanandam. “United They Fall: Bank Risk After the Financial Crisis.” Conditionally accepted, Review of Financial Studies.

Replication data and code for the paper, deposited at the Harvard Dataverse under DOI 10.7910/DVN/GOXYYT.

If you use these materials, please cite: Gandhi, Priyank, and Amiyatosh Purnanandam. “United They Fall: Bank Risk After the Financial Crisis.” Review of Financial Studies, forthcoming.

Download from Harvard Dataverse  (RFS_Dataverse.zip, about 693 MB)

Bank default risk estimates and MATLAB code

Nagel, S., and A. Purnanandam. “Banks’ Risk Dynamics and Distance-to-Default.” Review of Financial Studies, 2020, 33(6), 2421–2467.

MATLAB code implementing the model, together with estimates of five-year risk-neutral default probabilities for individual U.S. banks from 1987 to 2016.

If you use these materials, please cite: Nagel, Stefan, and Amiyatosh Purnanandam. “Banks’ Risk Dynamics and Distance-to-Default.” Review of Financial Studies 33, no. 6 (2020): 2421–2467.

Available on Stefan Nagel’s code and data page

TARP subsidy bank dataset

Flanagan, T., and A. Purnanandam. “Did Banks Pay ‘Fair’ Returns to Taxpayers on TARP?” Journal of Finance, 2024, 79, 2909–2941.

Estimated subsidies banks received from the TARP capital injection, bank by bank, for the entire sample of banks that received preferred equity injections from TARP.

If you use any part of the data, please cite: Flanagan, Thomas, and Amiyatosh Purnanandam. “Did Banks Pay Fair Returns to Taxpayers on TARP?” Journal of Finance 79, no. 5 (2024): 2909–2941.

Download the dataset  (FP_JF_data_web.xls)
